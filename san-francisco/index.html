Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Francisco

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and also shows higher bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in San Francisco

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, a musty smell at startup. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
